Pen & paper calibration

Pen positions

Pen endpoints have not been calibrated.

Pen in should clear the paper. Pen out should just touch it—not push hard. Support the gondola while adjusting and stop before any mechanical binding.

Paper corners

No saved calibration. Start below with the pen lifted.

Paper may be rotated. Jog the same pen-tip reference point to each corner: top-left → top-right → bottom-right → bottom-left. Drawings follow the four corners you capture. Do not move the gondola by hand during capture.

Jogs ramp up to 20 mm/s and are bounded to 50 mm per click; there is no automatic edge seeking. Check clearance before large jogs. Stop cancels motion and requires a reset. Keep the power switch accessible.

After reset, position is unknown. Jog to the exact saved top-left reference, or place it there before enabling motors. Then confirm below. Never restore at an arbitrary point.
