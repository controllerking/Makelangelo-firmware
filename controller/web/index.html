Pen & paper calibration

Pen positions

Pen endpoints have not been calibrated.

Pen in should clear the paper. Pen out should just touch it—not push hard. Support the gondola while adjusting and stop before any mechanical binding.

Paper corners

No saved calibration. Start below with the pen lifted.

Paper may be rotated. Jog the same pen-tip reference point to each corner: top-left → top-right → bottom-right → bottom-left. Drawings follow the four corners you capture. Do not move the gondola by hand during capture.

Jogs ramp up to 20 mm/s and are bounded to 50 mm per click; there is no automatic edge seeking. Check clearance before large jogs. Stop cancels motion and requires a reset. Keep the power switch accessible.

After reset, position is unknown. Your saved calibration already knows all four corners, so you only need to capture one: jog the pen-tip reference to any one saved corner (or place it there before enabling motors), choose that corner, then confirm below. Never restore at an arbitrary point, and not if the paper has moved.

Prepare your paper

PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG. Converted locally—never uploaded to a cloud service.

Color mode supports PNG, JPG and WebP. It draws one pen at a time, with black last. Each pen must have the same tip position and depth. Leave the paper and gondola fixed when swapping pens.

A practical balance of detail and drawing time.

Transparent areas become white paper. Outlines trace dark regions, not every edge in a photo.

Pen & toolpath settings

Travel speed remains within the robot’s existing limit. Increase it only after checking motion on your assembled machine. Zero gap bridging preserves highlights.

Y increases upward. Paper below the motor centres usually has a negative top Y. Measure the placement on your machine.
